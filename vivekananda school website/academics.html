Academic Programs

Structured classes from Pre-Primary to Senior Secondary in T. Nagar, Chennai.

Pre-Primary (Nursery – UKG)

Interactive early learning with stories, phonics, rhymes, art, and activity-based foundational skills.

Primary Wing (Classes 1 – 5)

Core focus on English, Tamil, Mathematics, Environmental Studies, and computer basics.

Middle School (Classes 6 – 8)

Concept-focused Science, Mathematics, Social Science, language skills, and lab sessions.

High & Higher Secondary (Classes 9 – 12)

Board exam preparation with regular tests, doubt-clearing sessions, and Science/Commerce streams.
